# Refine

Read the relevant projects' visions and state, [principles](../../docs/partner-principles.md) and relevant preferences (decks repository: `docs/mikes-deckbuilding-preferences.md`). Consult consolidated notes for relevant decisions, exclusions and open ideas. Follow [ManaBox workflow](../../docs/manabox-workflow.md) for phone edits, draft scope, major-pivot backups and verified delivery. Match depth to the question; iteration ends with a decision brief, not a mandatory full-deck report. This workflow supports any number of decks, not a particular project or paired-deck structure.

Refinement is an open loop: combine [exploration](../commander-explore/SKILL.md), [search](../commander-search/SKILL.md), draft edits/copies and [goldfishing](../commander-goldfish/SKILL.md) in whatever order helps. Test staged lists before publishing; existing draft authority covers these activities without another approval. Use goldfishing when actual sequencing can answer a consequential question about mana, curve or engine assembly, then revise the packages as needed. It is available during the pass, not a mandatory gate.

Use [enriched exports](../../docs/deck-artifacts.md) for card-complete analysis; resolve missing/disputed facts with [search](../commander-search/SKILL.md). Distinguish playing list from candidates, including real/proxy quantities and commanders. Bound incomplete analysis explicitly.

Find the bottleneck: development, color access, engine availability, card flow, interaction, recovery, setup or finish. Evaluate role timing/dependencies and unique slots separately; conditional draw, selection, rituals and late engines differ from reliable infrastructure. Review early colors/tapped-land costs alongside spells; pip ratios alone are insufficient. Role/land ranges are starting hypotheses, adjustable for commander, curve, strategy and pod.

For substantive passes, use [search](../commander-search/SKILL.md) to challenge the existing pool before settling on swaps. Search the desired outcome and missing role, not just the current engine's keyword: complementary Oracle wording, confirmed functional/art tags and adjacent resource or trigger interactions can reveal different solutions. Recently researched candidates remain useful, but repeated recycling or a persistent bottleneck calls for a new angle. Exact-name verification alone is not discovery. Respect explicit pool restrictions and keep prescribed edits narrow.

Compare promising outsiders against incumbents on fit, usable floor, dependencies and table experience. Apply Mike's [thematic resonance standard](../../docs/mikes-deckbuilding-preferences.md#thematic-resonance) to names, intended art and identity as well as mechanics, for both additions and existing cards in each branch; structural improvements must still belong in the deck. Keeping the old card is valid when it wins that comparison; overlap alone proves neither stagnation nor quality. Briefly surface a consequential discovery or the reason searched alternatives lost, rather than delivering a search report. Stop when the question is adequately tested or new routes stop adding useful options; disclose unavailable search instead of implying it happened.

Use [CLI refinements](../../integrations/manabox/README.md#iterative-refinements) as decisions happen: one `refine --file` call per substantive decision, not per turn or card pair. A pass may contain several decisions about engines, support counts, mana, themes or retained packages. Record finding/evidence, decision, reason, credible alternatives, tradeoffs and checks; distinguish predictions from measured results. Any number of adds, removals, moves or quantity changes may implement a decision, including none. Explain counts through usable timing, conditions and deck needs rather than nominal totals. A generic staple should face a thematic alternative. Preserve protected choices and explain conflicts.

Create/read/update/delete packages freely; `refine --update` and `--delete` recompute local state from the remaining packages. There is no required review-before-edit sequence. Use `--revert` when the reversal and its reason deserve to survive in the final account, or a compensating package when later edits overlap. Before publication, consider the sum of the decisions against each deck's vision, fun and preferences: does the combination still make sense, or has it drifted? Revise when needed, then record a brief assessment with `review --reason TEXT` (also supported with `--finalize`). This self-review binds to the current decisions/state; later edits make it stale. Quality warnings are advisory, not a checklist to satisfy by assertion. Structural checks do not validate colored mana: check actual sources, fetch targets, timing and competing costs against spell requirements, or explicitly mark that review incomplete.

Review the combined play pattern: visible/disruptable finish, agency, turn length, repetitive tutors/loops, recovery and political pile-ons. Basic `deck check` results are structural evidence, not a bracket/social certificate. [Failure checks](../../docs/guide-failure-modes.md) provide a quick diagnostic.

Use real-game feedback and simulator observations to test explanations against the project's intended experience. Identify the list revision, mulligans and relevant context; distinguish deck problems from piloting/tool errors and unusual draws. Seek repeated evidence and consider competing explanations: one missed land drop does not establish too few lands. Goldfishing tests development and sequencing; actual games and opponents' own feedback inform interaction, recovery and social experience. Record consequential findings and the next useful experiment in refinement decisions or consolidated notes.

Finalize through `review --finalize`, inspect the whole plan and publish with `review --finalize --plan FILE` following the workflow. Verified publication retains tool-managed history and clears the active pass. Render the returned `delivery_brief` directly as the primary chat response: decision, why, important alternatives/tradeoffs, and a short whole-pass assessment. Lightly consolidate superseded choices while keeping consequential reconsiderations visible. Follow the workflow's report guidance: reasoning belongs in chat, not behind an attachment or rewritten into a separate essay. Distill future-relevant conclusions into project notes; ManaBox notes remain the central pitch. Use the [original guide](../../docs/refinement-guide.md) only for deeper structural reference.
