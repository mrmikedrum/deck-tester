Focus: the board in motion. Look only at the frame sheets, zooms and trails, and follow every card, pile and page that moves on the table: from hand to stack, stack to battlefield or graveyard, tokens arriving and leaving piles, attackers and blockers, things dying or exiled, rows closing up. Narrate first, citing frames; compare adjacent frames part by part. Then judge against real cards on a real table and a player trying to follow the game: pops, snaps, teleports, paths nothing real would take, passing through other things, moving without reason, regrouping that loses track of a card, or leaving the player unsure what happened, whose it was, or what it targeted. Ignore the HTML around the board.
