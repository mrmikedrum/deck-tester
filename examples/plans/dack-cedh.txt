Fast mana into an early commander. Play fast mana (Sol Ring, Mana Vault, moxen, monoliths, Ancient Tomb)
as soon as you can and cast Dack Fayden as early as possible; his entry hands each opponent a goaded
creature from your library. Stax and protection pieces (Rule of Law, Trinisphere, Silence and the like) do
little against these opponents; cast them only with mana to spare after developing. Tutors should find mana
or more development. Removal has no targets: hold it.
