Manufactured Cosmos III — Atraxa / Assimilation

Grow an organized artifact fleet, with Borg production, Incubators and charge infrastructure as linked
but distinct engines. Proliferate advances counters already present; it cannot seed an empty Borg army.
A working charge-powered factory is useful even if its tokens have no +1/+1 counters. Do not confuse
that route with creature-counter payoffs, or make every card wait for both routes to be assembled.

Keep colours, early development and a follow-up, not merely a land count. Two to four lands is only
a first filter; account for tapped worlds and W/U/B/G fixing. The first Commander mulligan is free;
judge the already-bottomed hand and bound further shrinking. Fetch typed duals for missing colours,
not reflexive Forests. A productive start without every Atraxa colour is acceptable with real fixing.

Copper Myr makes G, Gold Myr W and Silver Myr U after summoning sickness wears off. They are artifact
casts/entries as well as fixing, but counters do not increase their mana output. Preserve a mana body
when station would prevent a useful play. Ordinary noncreature Incubators cannot station as creatures.

Set up Chamber before future artifact CASTS, and Conquest before future NONTOKEN artifact entries when
that does not skip needed mana. Tokens and Incubator transformations do not trigger either input.
Chamber's four-mana/tap activation creates Borg without removing its charges. Throne can recycle a
spare body to proliferate infrastructure; do not sacrifice essential mana or the last working engine.
Steel Overseer seeds artifact CREATURES, not untransformed Incubators. Filigree/Ouroboroid and other
actual counter placement can support army-wide growth; The Borg Queen's static +2/+2 is not counters.

Harmonize provides three cards without an engine. Insight Engine can build its own charge reserve
and draw from it; deploy it rather than repeatedly spending spare mana on small upgrades. Bankbuster
needs charges and paid activations, not crew, to draw. Research Craft needs 3 station counters BEFORE
future artifact casts draw and add charges. Co-Option Adjutant's card flow needs combat connections.
Inspiring Call and Terrasymbiosis need actual creature +1/+1-counter support; a counterless Borg swarm
or charge-only proliferation does not supply that. Note dormant payoffs without calling productive
factory turns dead. Decline optional draws where a finite closing line makes more cards unnecessary.

Cast Atraxa once fixing and useful counters/pressure justify her. Vigilance allows attacking, then
stationing afterward; stationing first loses that attack. Summoning sickness does not prevent being
station crew. Sword can seed counters, proliferate on connection and make Atraxa an independent
commander-damage threat. Her end-step proliferation can also grow Chamber, draw batteries and worlds.
Loading Zone doubles counters only on creatures, Spacecraft and Planets, not ordinary artifacts like
Chamber or Insight. Grow Incubators before transforming; Glissa can transform the existing batch at
combat without paying two for every token. New tokens still need to become creatures and lose sickness.

Sphere grants the artifact fleet flying at 6 charges; Locutus and Adjutant reward DAMAGE connections,
not simply declarations of attack. Pay for station thresholds that do something now, not endless crew.
Warp Speed changes tapped-land entries; its draw discount affects generic mana, not UU. Genesis Device
is a land swap with real fixing/entry payoffs, not an increase in land count. Free-cast X spells use
X=0; take them to hand when the effect permits and paying a useful X later is better.

Finish with the fleet, Queen's pump, Biomass Mutation, repeated counters from Expansion Algorithm, or
real evasive commander pressure as the board permits. Expansion's X proliferations need existing
counters; they do not create the first counter. Literal assimilation from Gift, Assimilate the Wounded,
Cube or Death Is Irrelevant is bounded by actual targets and costs. Keep recovery tied to real graveyard
targets; opposing graveyard exile can strand it, and creature exile is not a death-draw opportunity.

In multiplayer, save counters/removal and protection for meaningful opposing plays. Sunfall also
exiles your own supporting creatures; Organic Extinction spares artifacts, not Atraxa or nonartifact
support. In passive goldfishes, do not wipe only your own board just to spend mana. Evaluate actual
turn-by-turn useful options and interruptions, not a goldfish win rate or only the final fleet size.
