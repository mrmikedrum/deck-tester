Manufactured Cosmos — Atraxa / Assimilation pilot

Goal: develop an organized, increasingly uniform artifact fleet, with proliferation connecting worlds,
Incubators, charge counters and crew. Keep Borg and Phyrexian expressions together. This goldfish tests
assembly and resource competition, NOT win rate, opponents' agency or removal quality.

Keep a hand with usable early mana, a development action and a follow-up; 2–4 lands is only a first
filter. Account for tapped worlds and the four W/U/B/G colors Atraxa needs. A hand missing a commander
color is fine with real fixing and productive plays. First multiplayer mulligan is free. Fetch typed
lands for actual missing colors, particularly blue/black, rather than reflexively getting a Forest.

Early: fixing and cheap artifact infrastructure; put Maturation Chamber/Conquest Mission/Brimaz ahead of
future artifact CASTS/ENTRIES when doing so doesn't skip needed mana. Maturation triggers on casts,
Conquest on nontoken artifact entries: Incubator tokens and transformations do not seed those engines.
Get independent card access rather than relying only on expensive, combat-dependent Borg draw.

Middle: cast Atraxa once the four colors are functional and counters/crew make her useful. Her end-step
proliferation requires existing counters: seed Cornucopia, Chamber, spacecraft, Incubators, countered
creatures first. Spread upgrades across several useful components. Grow Incubators before paying two
to transform them; do not transform every token immediately and starve development. Standardized Borg
can attack with Locutus or Co-Option Adjutant for production/cards. Sphere's fleet-wide flying needs 6
charge counters and matters only with a fleet to carry it.

Combat/station: attack with vigilant Atraxa, then use her to station afterward if mana development no
longer needs her. Other attackers cannot also be tapped as station crew. Station Research Craft before
subsequent artifact casts if that enables draw. Don't repeatedly spend station activations after a
useful threshold without a reason. Use Steel Overseer/proliferation to organize the board, not a loop
that consumes all future choices. Throne of Geth should recycle a disposable artifact when proliferation
creates more value, not sacrifice essential mana or the last relevant engine.

Resource choices: Chamber costs four mana per activation; prefer building an engine or casting an
artifact over a tiny activation that delays the fleet. Borg combat draw is conditional, not guaranteed
against real opponents. Warp Speed can fix a land to hand and override tapped entries; its later draw
activation still needs UU after distinct-world discount. Genesis Device is a land swap, not ramp; use
only when landfall, fixing/world selection or untapped-entry payoff justifies it. Discovery effects that
cast X spells for free get X=0; take them to hand when appropriate.

Late: turn assembled infrastructure into a visible fleet finish with Cyberdrive or repeated production,
not opponent-locking or poison dependence. Cyberdrive does not give haste: existing artifacts can attack,
newly made ones may be summoning-sick. Keep removal/counters and protection for real threats; don't
spend them pointlessly in this passive scenario. Creature-only Sunfall spares noncreature Incubators,
but it still erases supporting creatures; a goldfish cannot establish recovery or interaction adequacy.

Refined sequencing: Decode Transmissions supplies two cards for three mana without needing a fleet.
Void is a bonus after a real nonland permanent departure (e.g. a useful artifact sacrifice), not a
reason to sacrifice essential infrastructure. Do not cast Sunfall into only your own developed board
in this passive test. Preserve reaction slots in the list; lack of goldfish targets is not evidence
that interaction is dispensable. With active Ezuri, landfall and Atraxa, prioritize starting production
then growing Incubators together before Glissa transforms them; avoid paying individually when her
combat mode can transform the batch. Favor concrete ship thresholds over indefinite stationing.
