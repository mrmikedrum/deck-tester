Living Worlds — Marath / Nature exploration pilot

Goal: develop a visibly varied living galaxy through land discovery, creatures, tokens and proliferation.
This is a passive-opponent goldfish, not a damage race or a reason to spend reactive cards pointlessly.
Keep Song of the Worldsoul as a supported late pleasure. Do not chase an infinite combo or a single
scripted line; build the resources the actual draw offers.

Keep 2–4 lands ONLY if their colors/tapped timing cast early development and a follow-up; a five-land
hand needs real card access. First multiplayer mulligan is free. Prioritize functional G, then W/R for
Marath. Fetch typed fixing for the hand, not just any green source. Make your land drop before spending
mana unless landfall sequencing or an additional-land effect gives a concrete reason to wait.

Early: cheap ramp/fixing, independent card flow, and a repeatable exploration/token/counter engine.
Pike opens top-library lands; use permitted extra land drops from Janeway. Seed counters on
Incubation Druid before expecting three mana. Preserve creatures as mana/board where station would
consume the turn's development. A spacecraft without enough crew is a future payoff, not current ramp.

Middle: cast Marath when its counters can support life, card flow or station, rather than mandating T3.
His activation's X is at least 1. Use counters to seed multiple resources, or make a useful real base-sized
Elemental; don't burn all his counters for trivial damage against 40-life passive opponents. Proliferate
then becomes shared growth. Attack safely to trigger Wormhole/Discovery/Apparition before tapping crew
after combat; creatures without vigilance cannot both attack and station that turn. Prioritize evolution
and branch-specific experiences over adding redundant counter consumers.

Use landfall intentionally: hold a fetch until Evolution Sage/Felidar Retreat can benefit when the delay
costs no land drop. Genesis Device's activation is a land swap, not net ramp; activate for a real payoff
from multiple land entries or better worlds, and remember the new lands lose old counters. Cast Discovery
with the crew to reach 6 over time. Discovering an X spell for free has X=0: put it into hand unless zero
is genuinely useful. Commander casting is not an automatic priority over establishing a draw engine.

Late: grow a diverse board and make a telegraphed burst. Song populates ORIGINAL token characteristics,
not added counters: real base-sized Marath Elementals, Beasts or Golems are good; a 0/0 Fractal wearing
counters is not. Preserve a creature spell for Song's first useful trigger when feasible. Unbound
Flourishing doubles X sorceries/casts as appropriate, not every counter placed. Hold removal/protection
in an empty goldfish; do not erase your board with Sunfall merely to spend mana. Report stalls or awkward
choices instead of disguising them with arbitrary overrides.

Refined sequencing: an available fetchland is an early green source, not a reason to play Plains for
several turns with green spells stranded. Tannuk is a three-mana exploration payoff: cast before the
land drop when mana already permits it, and aim for the SECOND landfall resolution (normal land plus
fetch/Wormhole/Frontier) for one card. A third landfall does not draw again. Wormhole's Planet tokens
are lands, not creature tokens, so they do not charge Staff of the Storyteller. Inspiring Call is also
card flow; use a developed countered board to replenish rather than holding it only as protection.

Live-playtest revision: the bottleneck was abundant mana but too few cards, with redundant recovery.
The list now cuts Cosmic Rebirth, Unnatural Restoration, Exploration Broodship, Eternity Elevator,
Generous Patron and Marketback Walker. Evolution Witness remains the recovery slot. Two Forests
become Mountains (eight Forests, three Mountains); fetch colors for actual costs, including RR.
Prioritize new card supply over more mana when the hand is thin. Wrenn's Resolve is early two-card
access; Escape to the Wilds offers five until next turn, with an extra land THIS turn. Commune with
Lava is an XRR instant: budget X against the mana you will need to play what it reveals; it is best
with next turn's mana still available, not automatically all-in at the start of a main phase.
Exploratory Mission goes before land entries and reveals a nonland for THIS turn only. Proliferate
its quest counters when useful; RR is added on a landfall resolution once it has five counters.
Elemental Bond draws on power-three entries, not later growth. Marath can turn three counters and
three mana into an entering 3/3 Elemental and a draw, while retaining his last counter if possible.
Shamanic Revelation refills from the whole ecosystem, not just countered or power-four creatures.
Do not chase arbitrary lethal damage in the passive goldfish: watch whether red access becomes
usable spells and whether mana/crew choices leave those cards stranded past their deadlines.

Evaluation correction: score card ACCESS by cards actually cast/played or retained in hand, not by
access spells cast. Before every main-phase hand play, check playable exile options and their deadline.
Use an exiled land in preference to an equivalent land in hand; keep useful hand cards for later.
Spend mana on usable expiring spells before optional adapt, station or fresh ramp. Exceptions require a
concrete better line, not habit. Once mana is plentiful, replenishment and varied life beat more ramp.
Wildspeaker is a refill only with a substantial NON-HUMAN; aim for at least four cards, not a routine
five-mana draw-two. Build the body first and retain enough mana to draw. Adapt with counters already on
Evolution Witness does nothing. Against passive opponents keep real interaction instead of spending
it pointlessly. A full hand or big board is an observation, not proof of multiplayer deck quality.

Current test iteration replaces Scoutlander and Cornucopia with Riker and Bankbuster, not the
successful refills. Riker is a red-white exploration officer: haste means three mana plus one yields
two cards immediately; vigilance lets him attack and draw afterward. Do not station away his draw
when the hand is short. Choose an opponent deliberately: they get two cards, too. Bankbuster draws
without crew for two mana and a charge; use proliferation to preserve this card reserve. A spare
three-power body may crew it, but protecting mana and useful engines comes first. Retain the burst
refills rather than expecting these once-per-tap engines alone to replenish a late empty hand.

Do NOT confuse using exile permissions with profitable access: if Mission exiles Emergency Eject
and opponents have no nonland targets, let it expire. Never destroy your own Loading Zone merely to
obtain a Lander. Count only useful converted cards when evaluating the draw package. Unsupported
aggression or wasted adaptation is a pilot flaw, not evidence that a draw/ramp card should be cut.

Final mana test: one Plains becomes War Room (five Plains remain; thirty-eight total lands).
War Room is the unconditional-on-board fallback when a thin hand has no active engine: three mana,
tap it, pay three life, draw one. It is not the main draw plan and should not displace a profitable
Riker/Bankbuster activation or a real refill. Shamanic Revelation for one body is another five-mana
cantrip trap: grow the ecosystem first unless a specific emergency makes the low payout necessary.

Riker is selective help, not an always-on group-hug engine. If the hand is already stocked, cast
useful cards instead of automatically giving an opponent two more. Especially do not draw simply
to discard at cleanup. Repeated activation in a passive goldfish does not validate that political
cost at a real table; the conditioned reference intentionally checks availability, not optimal play.
