Living Worlds — Marath / Nature exploration pilot

Goal: develop a visibly varied living galaxy through land discovery, creatures, tokens and proliferation.
This is a passive-opponent goldfish, not a damage race or a reason to spend reactive cards pointlessly.
Keep Song of the Worldsoul as a supported late pleasure. Do not chase an infinite combo or a single
scripted line; build the resources the actual draw offers.

Keep 2–4 lands ONLY if their colors/tapped timing cast early development and a follow-up; a five-land
hand needs real card access. First multiplayer mulligan is free. Prioritize functional G, then W/R for
Marath. Fetch typed fixing for the hand, not just any green source. Make your land drop before spending
mana unless landfall sequencing or an additional-land effect gives a concrete reason to wait.

Early: cheap ramp/fixing, independent card flow, and a repeatable exploration/token/counter engine.
Pike opens top-library lands; use permitted extra land drops from Janeway/Broodship. Seed counters on
Incubation Druid before expecting three mana. Preserve creatures as mana/board where station would
consume the turn's development. A spacecraft without enough crew is a future payoff, not current ramp.

Middle: cast Marath when its counters can support life, card flow or station, rather than mandating T3.
His activation's X is at least 1. Use counters to seed multiple resources, or make a useful real base-sized
Elemental; don't burn all his counters for trivial damage against 40-life passive opponents. Proliferate
then becomes shared growth. Attack safely to trigger Wormhole/Discovery/Apparition before tapping crew
after combat; creatures without vigilance cannot both attack and station that turn. Prioritize evolution
and branch-specific experiences over adding redundant counter consumers.

Use landfall intentionally: hold a fetch until Evolution Sage/Felidar Retreat can benefit when the delay
costs no land drop. Genesis Device's activation is a land swap, not net ramp; activate for a real payoff
from multiple land entries or better worlds, and remember the new lands lose old counters. Cast Discovery
with the crew to reach 6 over time. Discovering an X spell for free has X=0: put it into hand unless zero
is genuinely useful. Commander casting is not an automatic priority over establishing a draw engine.

Late: grow a diverse board and make a telegraphed burst. Song populates ORIGINAL token characteristics,
not added counters: real base-sized Marath Elementals, Beasts or Golems are good; a 0/0 Fractal wearing
counters is not. Preserve a creature spell for Song's first useful trigger when feasible. Unbound
Flourishing doubles X sorceries/casts as appropriate, not every counter placed. Hold removal/protection
in an empty goldfish; do not erase your board with Sunfall merely to spend mana. Report stalls or awkward
choices instead of disguising them with arbitrary overrides.

Refined sequencing: an available fetchland is an early green source, not a reason to play Plains for
several turns with green spells stranded. Tannuk is a three-mana exploration payoff: cast before the
land drop when mana already permits it, and aim for the SECOND landfall resolution (normal land plus
fetch/Wormhole/Frontier) for one card. A third landfall does not draw again. Wormhole's Planet tokens
are lands, not creature tokens, so they do not charge Staff of the Storyteller. Inspiring Call is also
card flow; use a developed countered board to replenish rather than holding it only as protection.
If Marath dies into the graveyard and Cosmic Rebirth is used, choose hand, not battlefield: a free
return enters with zero mana spent and no counters. Do not count that route as a new counter engine.
