Ramp, then grow. Make a land drop every turn and cast mana rocks, mana creatures and land fetchers first.
Cast Marath (R/G/W) as soon as you have its colours; it enters with a +1/+1 counter per mana spent and later
spends them to grow creatures or make X/X tokens. Then develop creatures, counters and tokens, since
proliferate and populate need them on the board. Spend your mana every turn. Removal and protection do
nothing against these opponents: hold them.
