Living Worlds III — Marath / Growing Cosmos

Build an expansive, varied living board through lands, +1/+1 counters, creature tokens and proliferation.
Song of the Worldsoul is the nature branch's late populate pleasure, not a requirement to force every
opening down the same line. Follow the actual draw. Growth comes first; naturally assembled Marath
combos can finish a developed game, but do not make every start a race for a scripted loop.

Opening: keep usable colours, early development and a follow-up. Two to four lands is a first filter,
not a substitute for checking tapped timing and access to green, then Marath's red/white. The first
Commander mulligan is free; judge the already-bottomed hand and do not mulligan indefinitely. Fetch
Temple Garden/Stomping Ground/Sacred Foundry as the hand needs, not always a basic Forest.

Early development includes Copper Myr (G), Gold Myr (W), Iron Myr (R), Ornithopter, Signet and land
search. Myr cannot tap for mana while summoning-sick, and +1/+1 counters do not multiply their mana.
Scholar of New Horizons trades any permanent counter for a Plains card: typed duals qualify. It puts
that land onto the battlefield tapped only while an opponent has more lands; otherwise it supplies
hand/land-drop access. Preserve survival counters and useful spacecraft/engine thresholds.

Choose between new mana and new cards based on the actual bottleneck. Harmonize is independent draw;
Staff starts with a Spirit and can spend its story counters for cards. Bankbuster draws without crew
and proliferation can refill its charges. War Room costs three mana, tap and three life with Marath.
Wildspeaker needs worthwhile non-Human power. Tribute and Terrasymbiosis are conditional engines:
Terrasymbiosis wants a large +1/+1-counter placement on a creature, once per turn, not charge growth.
A paid Marath recast may refill through it. Direct reanimation has zero mana spent and normally needs
an extra-counter replacement to keep Marath alive. Evaluate actual graveyard targets for Cosmic Rebirth.

Cast Mission before useful land entries. Its exile permission lasts THIS turn; reaching five quest
counters through proliferation alone does not add RR, but a resolving landfall trigger at five does.
Wrenn's Resolve, Escape and Commune access lasts through your next turn; Escape's extra land allowance
lasts only this turn. Count cards actually played, not access spells cast. Spend on USEFUL expiring
cards before optional sinks, but do not destroy your own engine merely to use expiring removal.

Broodship grants an extra land play at 3 charges; at 8 its once-per-your-turn permanent cast from the
graveyard also sacrifices a land and pays normal costs. Genesis Device swaps lands, not net ramp.
Use those world entries with Mission, Evolution Sage and Felidar Retreat; Wormhole's Planet is a land
token, not a creature token for Staff. Attack when useful, then station with untapped bodies; ordinary
attackers cannot also crew that turn. Avoid indefinite stationing beyond meaningful thresholds.

Marath seeds useful creatures, makes base-sized Elementals, removes threats and can close the game.
Keep enough counters to survive when needed. Druid needs a counter before making three mana. Scholar
can spend a different permanent's counter instead of consuming a vital one. Walking Ballista is XX:
paying four normally gives X=2. Unbound doubles X for permanent spells and copies X spells/abilities;
it does not double Marath's fixed-cost entry counters. X=0 is normally empty/dead unless a concrete
counter replacement or trigger makes it useful; free-cast effects also set X=0.

Cryptic Trilobite makes ability-only CC, not spell mana. With Marath and an extra-counter replacement,
its repeatable counter economy can support a genuine closing loop; keep both 0/0 bodies alive and
account for opposition. Ballista can turn accumulated counters into removal/burn without tapping.
Song copies BASE token characteristics, not added counters: real Elementals, Beasts or Golems are
useful; a counter-sized 0/0 Alien is not. A creature spell after Song can start that populate engine.

In multiplayer, use removal/protection against actual threats and note commander damage separately
from life (21 combat damage from one commander is lethal). In passive goldfishes, hold interaction
rather than wiping your own board without a real benefit. Track stranded dependencies, colour locks,
missed good plays and productive activation-only turns separately; end-board size is not a flow verdict.
