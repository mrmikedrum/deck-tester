Blue creatures and copies. Make a land drop every turn and cast Sol Ring early. Springleaf Drum only makes mana by tapping a creature, so cast creatures first. Princess Yue is cheap: cast her early; when she dies she returns as a land named Moon. Clones and copy effects (Phantasmal Image, Phyrexian Metamorph, Vizier of Many Faces and the like) are best copying your best creature. Tap and untap tricks and repeated enter-the-battlefield triggers grow a large board; attack with it. Counterspells and bounce have no targets here: hold them, and draw cards with spare mana.
