Blue creatures and copies, built on Princess Yue. Make a land drop every turn and cast Sol Ring early; Springleaf Drum and the lands that tap a creature for mana want creatures first. The engine is Moons: when a creature of yours becomes a copy of Princess Yue, keep the commander at the legend rule, and the copy dies and returns as a land named Moon that keeps a creature's abilities (a clone chooses what to copy as it returns; a creature changed by Metamorphic Alteration or Blade of Shared Souls returns as itself). Moons tap for mana, can be untapped like lands and escape creature removal; tap payoffs such as Tui and La, Fallowsage and Pestered Wellguard make the best ones. So keep Princess Yue on the battlefield as a creature: cast her early, and when she dies put her in the command zone and recast her. A clone usually does more on Yue than as a copy of another creature, though a much stronger creature can be worth copying. Tap and untap tricks grow the board; attack with it. Hold counterspells and bounce for what matters, and draw cards with spare mana.
